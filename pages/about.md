# About Me

<p>Born and raised in New Zealand, where I graduated with a Bachelor of Pharmacy from the University of Otago in 2016. My career has since taken me across healthcare, real estate and business operations.</p>
<p>I moved to London in 2024 and now work as a Facilities Operations Coordinator at Imperial Business School. In 2026, I received the Rising Star (Professional Services) award at the Business School Staff Awards.</p>
<p>I've always enjoyed problem-solving, critical thinking and identifying opportunities to improve how things work. Having experienced the limitations of healthcare systems first hand, I'm particularly interested in how data can support meaningful improvements.</p>
<p>I'm currently completing a Professional Certificate in Data Analytics, developing skills in Python, SQL, statistical modelling and data visualisation. My capstone project explores the relationship between delayed hospital discharges and A&E waiting times in the NHS. </p>
<p>I'm interested in opportunities that combine my healthcare background, operational experience and analytical skills to address complex problems and support better decision-making.</p>
